**Evidence levels**
- ★★★ **Proven:** used in a job with a concrete, quantified result. Safe to lead with.
- ★★ **Working:** used in a job without a strong metric, or used only in the past. Fine to list.
- ★ **Familiar:** coursework, certificate only, or claimed on a CV with no supporting bullet. "Familiar with" at most. ⚠️ means it needs a story before you lean on it.
- ✗ **Not done:** keep the row with the date it was corrected, so it is never claimed.
